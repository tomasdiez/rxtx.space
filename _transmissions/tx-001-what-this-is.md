A transmission is not a publication. A publication is what's left after the reviewers, the edits, the sanding — an idea made safe enough to quote. A transmission is earlier than that. It's the idea while the solder is still hot, sent out to find out whether anyone else can hear it.

That's what this log is: numbered essays, dated, written in public, released before they're ready. "Ready" is where thinking goes to retire.

I've watched good ideas die of politeness — circulated privately, refined endlessly, published never. So the rule here is the opposite. Put the draft on air with a number and a date, let it be wrong in specific ways, and fix it where people can watch. Drafts before doctrine.

Expect essays about cities, about tools, about sound, about the slow and unglamorous business of change. Expect them to contradict each other sometimes. A log is not a position paper; it's a record of where the signal was on a given night.

Some of these will graduate. The ones that survive contact with other people's thinking get rewritten properly and filed in the permanent record at [tomasdiez.com](https://www.tomasdiez.com) — citations, context, a haircut. That site is the archive. This one is the antenna.

If you want transmissions as they land, [use the feed]({{ "/transmissions.xml" | relative_url }}). No algorithm decides whether you see it, and no platform sits in the middle counting you. RSS is a frequency you tune to on purpose.
