Experimental studio · Tomas Diez · Bali, Indonesia

RX is receive.
TX is transmit.

889296100104108 MHz

Radio people have trusted those two abbreviations for a century. Everything in this studio runs on the same two moves: take signal in, put signal out, be honest about the noise.

RXTX is not a portfolio. Portfolios are for finished things, and almost nothing here is finished. This is where I work out loud — music, design, art, changemaking — before any of it hardens into canon.

I grew up in Venezuela, spent years in Barcelona, and set up this bench in Uluwatu, Bali. Made from Bali, wired to everywhere.

This is a studio for the age of crisis. Institutions answer crisis with reports; a studio can answer with attempts — small, cheap, transmitted early, corrected in public. Some attempts will fail on air. Better that than dying in a drawer.

If you can read this, you're in range.
