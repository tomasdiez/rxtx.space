CH.02 · Sound

Signal path

From a small town in Venezuela to late nights in Uluwatu.

It started in a small town in Venezuela, with a grunge band. Loud, and completely serious about it.

Then we lost Guido, our drummer. A genius — the kind of musician who makes everyone around him sound better than they are, and you only measure it once he's gone. We tried to reinvent ourselves after that, many times. It was never the same.

Later we found our way back as rec.audio — less a band than a license to experiment together again. In time it split into separate projects, the way these things go.

These days the signal comes from Bali. I play live sets here — improvised, hardware and software talking over each other in real time — and I make bedroom productions under the name TX. Transmit. It was always going to be called that.
